Research PM

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Contact: sejinp98@gmail.com

Research PM is a personal research project manager built and used by Sejin Park only. It is not a public service and does not accept other users. It runs on a private server owned by the developer and is reachable only over the developer's private network.

Data accessed from Google

When the developer connects a Google account, Research PM requests the following permissions:

  • calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events.readonly: to read the list of calendars and events, so they can be shown in the app's research calendar.
  • calendar.app.created: to create one secondary calendar named "Research PM" and to add, update, and delete events only in that calendar.
  • openid and email: to display which account is connected.

How the data is used

  • Events are displayed in the app's calendar and used to schedule lab-meeting summaries for the developer.
  • Research schedules entered in the app (meetings, submission deadlines) are written to the "Research PM" calendar. Other calendars are never modified.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train machine-learning models.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the developer's private server. A copy of upcoming events is cached on the same server to display the calendar.
  • No data is shared with or transferred to any third party.

Retention and deletion

  • Cached events are refreshed periodically and are deleted when the Google account is disconnected in the app.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections. After revocation, stored tokens can no longer be used.

Research PM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
