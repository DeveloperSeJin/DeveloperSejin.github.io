I am a researcher at Affctiv, working at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience and deep learning. My work now centers on human–AI interaction: I design agents that perceive speech, text, and vision, and build sLLM-based social robots that engage children on the autism spectrum through interactive, real-world tasks. Earlier, I worked on EEG-to-text decoding — translating raw brain signals into natural language.
